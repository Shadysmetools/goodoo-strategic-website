Goodoo.ai Go-to-Market Strategy

A Targeted 12-Month Plan to Capture the Initial Market

Core Strategy: Spearhead with High-Trust Channels

The GTM strategy for the first 12 months is centered on a 'spearhead' model. We will leverage the high-trust, high-relevance channels of the CEO's personal network and the Belgian Odoo annual event to acquire a critical mass of ideal first users. The goal is not broad awareness, but deep penetration and validation within the Odoo PM community. This initial traction will fuel all future expansion.

Months 1-3 (Q3 2025) (Pre-Launch & Event Preparation)

Founder's Circle Email Campaign

Target the CEO's list of ~500 Odoo PMs. This is not a cold email blast; it's a personal invitation to an exclusive circle.

Actions:
  • Draft a 3-part email sequence
  • Email 1 (Teaser): Announce a new tool for Odoo PMs, highlighting the core pain point of project memory loss
  • Email 2 (Demo): Share a compelling, 2-minute video demonstrating the visual artifact generation
  • Email 3 (Invite): Invite them to join the 'Founder's Circle' beta with an exclusive offer
Objective: Secure 50-100 sign-ups for the beta waitlist before the event

Odoo Event Shock & Awe Package

Prepare materials to make a significant impact at the Belgian Odoo event in September. The goal is to be the most talked-about new tool at the conference.

Actions:
  • Develop event collateral
  • Live Demo Script: A polished, 5-minute demo script focusing on the 'screen-to-guide' workflow
  • Event-Specific Landing Page: A simple page with a QR code for attendees to scan, offering an exclusive 'Odoo Event' deal
  • One-Pager Handout: A visually appealing summary of the problem, solution, and core features
Objective: Be fully prepared to capture leads, provide stunning demos, and convert attendees into beta users on-site

Months 4-6 (Q4 2025) (Launch Execution & Early Adopter Program)

High-Impact Event Presence

Execute the plan at the Odoo event. The focus is on active engagement, not passive presence.

Actions:
  • Actively network and drive traffic to your booth/presence
  • Run the live demo continuously
Objective: Acquire at least 50 high-quality beta sign-ups directly from the event. Generate significant buzz within the Odoo partner and developer community.

White-Glove Onboarding Program

Treat the first 100 users (from emails and the event) like VIPs. Their success is your success.

Actions:
  • Personally onboard every single beta user via a 1-on-1 video call. Help them set up their first project and record their first meeting
  • Create a private Discord server or Slack channel for the 'Founder's Circle' to provide direct support and gather unfiltered feedback
Objective: Achieve a >70% activation rate (users who record at least 3 meetings) for the initial beta cohort. Identify your first 5-10 power users who can become case studies.

Months 7-12 (Q1-Q2 2026) (Amplify, Expand & Scale)

Amplify with Case Studies

Turn early adopter success into powerful marketing assets.

Actions:
  • Interview your top 5-10 power users
  • Create detailed case studies (blog posts and short videos) showcasing how they use Goodoo.ai to solve specific problems
Objective: Develop a library of social proof to use in future marketing, on the website, and for sales enablement.

Expand to New Channels

Use the initial traction to expand into broader channels.

Actions:
  • Begin targeted content marketing. Write blog posts about Odoo project management challenges, and feature Goodoo.ai as the solution
  • Launch targeted LinkedIn ad campaigns at 'Project Managers' in the software industry, using case studies as the primary creative
Objective: Establish a repeatable, scalable channel for lead generation beyond the initial spearhead channels.
